Using the data elsewhere

The download has one row per site or app, day, hour and counter: app, day, hour_utc, name, n. Day and hour are in UTC. Open it in a spreadsheet, give it to any AI, or read it from a script.

The same data is at https://moc7-ask.sofiag-gallego.workers.dev/stats?key=YOUR_KEY&format=csv, with optional &from=YYYY-MM-DD&to=YYYY-MM-DD&app=games. A spreadsheet can load it directly, for example in Google Sheets with =IMPORTDATA("…").

Counter names: open one opening of a page or app · page:… which page · view:…, tab:…, act:… tabs and buttons · open:phone|tablet|desktop, open:installed|browser, scheme:dark|light, lang:…, country:… · games: play:…, win:…, loss:…, solved:…, best:…, minute:… (minutes played), ev:game:event, player:new and player:back-… (days since this browser last played).
