ENFRES

Art

"Déjame"

"Déjame" is my first official song. This song reflects my journey through life, capturing moments of hope, struggle, and the pursuit of beauty amidst uncertainty.

As a singer and actress, I have played numerous musicals, operas, and plays since I was 10 years old.

As a violinist, I toured with my school orchestra across Europe in 2001, and that same year I competed in a national violin contest. I later performed with the Universidad de La Serena symphonic orchestra, the university orchestra in Santiago, and several ensembles. With the group Magreb, we released the album "Música Andalusí", sponsored by the Embassy of Morocco in Chile (2011).

Between 2009 and 2011 I studied lyrical singing at the Institute of Music, Pontificia Universidad Católica de Chile, while completing my astronomy degree. From 2009 to 2015, I worked as a singer and violinist at weddings, graduations, and corporate events, which helped finance my studies. Later, while at ETH Zurich, I joined a student band and performed at several events.

In 2019 I founded my own amateur musical theater company, ZIMT (Zurich International Musical Theater).

Following my passion for arts, science, and communication, in 2021 I created the YouTube channel Cosmosofia.

I have also kept a personal blog since 2008 with poetry and reflections: nefinia.blogspot.com.
