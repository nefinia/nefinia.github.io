What can Cosmoventures help you solve?

  • You are building an AI, educational or deeptech product and need its scientific claims reviewed.
  • You need a literature review, feasibility assessment or scientific roadmap.
  • You are producing a video, podcast, exhibition or article and need scientific accuracy.
  • Your technical team needs to communicate complex ideas clearly to investors, clients or the public.
  • You need an original workshop, keynote or cultural format grounded in real science.
  • You are building or deploying AI systems and need an independent view of how reliably they reason about science and the world.
