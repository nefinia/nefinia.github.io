About this map

Each point on the map is an idea from the course, and each line joins two ideas that help explain each other. You can read every line in both directions, and the two readings are different. Reading the fifth through roughness explains why the fifth sounds smooth; reading roughness through the fifth shows how musicians use beats to tune. The colours show the five lenses of the course: the physics of sound, mathematics, the ear and perception, tuning and harmony, and the traditions and arts of the world. 23

Tap an idea to read about it. To read one idea through another, drag it onto a linked idea, or tap the two one after the other. You can also choose a module to light up the ideas it covers, or a story to follow a short path across the map. Each idea links to the part of the course where it is explained.

Sources

    The readings were written for this map, and the numbers point to sources for the facts they use. Two of the sources are cited from memory and still need checking. This is one way to draw the map, and someone else could choose other ideas and other links.
