About this map

Each point of light is an idea from the course, and each line connects two ideas. Every connection can be read in both directions, and the two readings differ: the fifth seen through roughness is a different story from roughness seen through the fifth. Colours follow the five lenses of the course: the physics of sound, mathematics, the ear and perception, tuning and harmony, and the traditions and arts of the world. 23

Hover over a big term, or tap it, to see its neighbours. Drag one idea onto another, or tap two in turn, to read the second through the first. Choose a module to light up the ideas it covers, or a story to follow a path. Each idea links back to the place in the course where it is explained.

Sources

    The readings are written for this map. Each one puts a connection into words, and its numbers point to sources for the facts it uses. Which ideas appear, and how each link is worded, is a choice, and another person could draw the map differently.
