Colored XOR CMY XOR spells

Goal: make Your board match the Target exactly (shape and color). You place tetromino stamps. Stamping uses XOR (toggle): stamping the same CMY color on a cell twice cancels it back.

Color = 3-bit CMY state per cell. Each cell stores three on/off bits: Cyan (C), Magenta (M), Yellow (Y). A stamp flips exactly one bit (C or M or Y) over its footprint.
- One bit on: pure C/M/Y
- Two bits on: mix (C+M, C+Y, M+Y)
- Three bits on (C+M+Y): shown as dark/black
- Zero bits: empty/paper

Overlaps matter because XOR flips bits: if a cell already has Cyan and you stamp Cyan again, Cyan turns off.

How to play Colored XOR

Recreate the target exactly—every filled cell and every color. You can overlap stamps because XOR lets colors combine and cancel.

1. Pick a shape

Tap a tetromino to select it, use ⟳ to rotate it, or drag it directly toward the board.

2. Choose a color

Every stamp flips one color channel: Cyan, Magenta, or Yellow.

3. The XOR toggle rule

Stamping the same channel twice turns it back off: C ⊕ C = empty. This works the same way for M and Y.

4. Combine different channels

Different channels stay on together: C+M is purple, C+Y green, M+Y orange, and all three produce brown.

5. Match exactly

Solve when your board matches the target in both shape and color. Undo is always available.
