🧠 SensoryVerse Cognitive Games

These mini-games are experiments designed to explore how we focus, adapt, and remember — while having fun. They’re part of the evolving SensoryVerse platform. Play, reflect, and discover!

🔊 EchoPattern

A perception and memory challenge. Repeat color-sound sequences as they grow in complexity. Measures your visual, auditory, and mixed response styles.

Play EchoPattern

🎯 Sound Focus

A reaction game with rising sensory load. Can you stay focused across calm, medium, and chaotic audio environments? Tracks your accuracy and reaction time.

Play Sound Focus

♻️ Quick Reframe

A mental flexibility game. Follow changing rules quickly — shape, color, or background. Improves your ability to adapt and shift attention.

Play Quick Reframe

🧩 Memory Match

Challenge your memory! Match visual icons or words — but not both — and see whether you excel at image-based or word-based recall.

Play Memory Match
