About this map

The map starts from the programme of Models of Consciousness 7 in Copenhagen, 12 to 16 October 2026. Each point of light is an idea that appears in the sessions, and each line connects two ideas. Every connection can be read in both directions, and the two readings differ. 17, 18 Colours follow the conference themes of the MoC7 Atlas, with theories and formal models as one group. 19

Hover over a big term, or tap it, to see its neighbours. Drag one idea onto another, or tap two in turn, to read the second through the first.

Sources

    A dagger after a talk means the programme gives only its title, so the page uses nothing beyond the title. Talks without a dagger have an abstract in the programme. The programme was read on 14 October 2026 and may change.

    The readings are written for this map. Each one puts a connection into words, and its numbers point to the sources for the facts it uses. Which ideas appear, and how each link is worded, is a choice, and another person could draw the map differently. This is a first draft, and people who work on each theory would improve it.
