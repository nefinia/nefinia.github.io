Cosmoneo

“Cosmoneo” is an innovative educational tool designed to support interactive, in-depth learning of astronomical and scientific concepts.

Play online

Cosmoneo now has an online version on Sofi Games. Its rules follow real stellar physics: stars form from the gas around them, massive stars die young, supernovae sweep gas into shells rich in metals, and later generations of stars can form planets. It can be played against bots, in English, French or Spanish.

Board Game

As the project’s first product, Cosmoneo takes a playful approach to teaching the processes of the Universe, making it easier to learn about stellar evolution and more.

Components and Gameplay

It includes star and evolution cards, a hexagonal board, dice and gas coins that simulate the mass of gas. These components are used to represent and calculate the moves and transformations in the game, for an interactive and richly informative learning experience.

Conclusion

Beyond the game, Cosmoneo is an educational platform that deepens the understanding of astronomy and turns learning into an exciting, instructive adventure.
